# The Vigil (working title)

A third-person melee action game in Unreal Engine 5. A powerful country has been
quietly captured by a hidden Order, and a new student of the hunted Vigil must win
back both the land and its people's belief. The combat centers on the parry, with
a power curve that runs from heavy hits in the first fight to splitting mountains.

## Foundation design

The [Workspace card](../../workspace/2026-10-17_the-vigil/foundation.md) holds
the agreed foundation: hard rules, story, world and art direction, enemies,
combat, movement, progression, technical risks and open questions. Read it before
working on this project, and keep its hard rules in every asset and scene.
